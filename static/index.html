Method

Universe: NSE F&O stocks only. Results dates: NSE board-meeting intimations and the NSE event calendar (exchange filings); names that already filed are pruned. Numbers: NSE integrated XBRL filings (consolidated when available). Score /100: growth 25, analysts 15, positives 15, sector 10, history 15, momentum 10, room 10. Broker estimates are not available from free sources, so the score is computed out of 85 and rescaled. A ≥ 75 (growth and history ≥ 7); B 65–74; D = weak last quarter with hard recovery evidence (revenue up QoQ plus momentum or fresh orders). A/B exclude loss-makers, governance flags and market cap below Rs.5,000 cr. Pledge data: NSE pledge feed returned no data in testing, so pledge > 25% is not checked. Event signals come from non-routine NSE announcements the market can still react to (the last two sessions), with the attached PDF read for the actual content. Each signal is held for the period where the studies found an edge (news 2 sessions, follow-through 4, results and pre-results 5) and closes early at its target or stop.
